Our Story

T•N•T Threads & Bows was inspired by our two daughters, Teagan & Timberlyn.

During our 103-day NICU journey with our second daughter, I found comfort and purpose in creating a bow and children's item business filled with love.

With our sweet 2-year-old cheering me on at home, this business became a way to honor both of our girls and celebrate the little moments that mean the most.
